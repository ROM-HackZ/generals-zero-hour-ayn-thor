# Thor RTS: first public preview

Zero Hour on the AYN Thor, with buttons and both screens doing something useful.
The top screen is the battlefield; the bottom holds the map, selection, commands,
production and General's Powers. This is an early build, and there's more to test.

## In this build

- Left-stick camera movement, right-stick rotation and zoom, and a precision cursor.
- Command and group wheels with the game's own icons, readable labels and details.
- A command deck with portraits, health, costs, queues, cooldowns and power points.
- Original radar fog/availability and a camera outline on the map.
- Controller menus, text entry, remapping and camera settings.
- Full top-screen output, including the pause screen.

## Checked on the Thor

USA, China and GLA artwork, skirmish startup, both screens, both wheels and paging,
building placement, production cancellation/refunds, radar and General's Powers.
Local saves survived app updates and loaded again. The revised interface was
checked on the device and reads well. The observed game rate is about 30 fps.

Campaigns, Challenge, every ability/group combination, disconnect/reconnect,
single-screen fallback and long-session stability still need proper testing.
This release is for testing; it isn't a finished controller edition.

## Playing

Download `Thor-RTS-0.1.0-alpha.1.apk` below, and follow
[the installation guide](https://github.com/ROM-HackZ/generals-zero-hour-ayn-thor/blob/main/docs/HOWTO/THOR_INSTALL.md).
You need your own PC game data; it isn't in the APK. The Steam installation is
the one used for testing. Updates over the previous Thor test app keep its saves.

The source at this tag, build instructions and dependency pins are in the repo.
`SHA256SUMS.txt` identifies the downloads. Keep bug reports specific: what you
selected, what you pressed, and what happened.

Built on the community ports credited in
[the README](https://github.com/ROM-HackZ/generals-zero-hour-ayn-thor#credits),
with their license and copyright notices preserved.
